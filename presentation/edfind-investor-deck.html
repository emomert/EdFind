Executive summary

The whole story, on one slide.

EdFind is an AI-powered education advisory platform helping Turkish students discover, compare, and apply to European master’s programs with clarity and confidence.

Our research validated a clear pain point: all 13 interviewees described the process as fragmented, time-consuming, and unreliable. Students juggle scattered university websites, inconsistent AI chat outputs, WhatsApp groups, and costly consultants — and still risk missing the right programs.

EdFind solves this through three integrated layers: a personalized AI recommendation engine, a country-specific application toolkit, and a verified student community with real reviews and program-level guidance.

The model is B2B2C: student subscriptions plus university-side visibility and recruitment. Early validation is strong — 9 of 13 interviewees preferred full access, with willingness to pay clustering at 2,000–5,000 TL.

The problem

Five ways the current process fails students.

✕Time-consuming research across fragmented, scattered sources
✕No personalized guidance — generic lists, not your fit
✕Expensive dependency on consultants (€500–2,000)
✕Universities are nearly impossible to compare like-for-like
✕Hidden bias — advisors push partner schools for commission

Our solution

Personalized AI guidance, centralized data, and verified student insight — in one place.

AI-powered matching

A 14-question profile (or plain-language search) ranks your best-fit programs with a personal, honest rationale for each.

Smart comparison

Structured, verified data — tuition, rankings, deadlines, housing costs — compared side by side.

Process tracking

Application tracker with deadlines, task board, and AI-drafted CVs & cover letters.

Centralized hub

Everything in one place — 196 programs, 58 universities, 15 countries, with housing and cost-of-living data.

Verified community

Reviews and answers only from .edu-verified students and alumni — no spam, no anonymous noise.
